Frequently asked

Do I need a paid Notion plan?

No. Everything works on Notion's free plan, on desktop, web and mobile. You just need a free Notion account.

How do I get the template after I buy?

Right after checkout you'll get an email with your template link and the PDF guide. Open the link, click "Duplicate", and it's copied into your Notion workspace. It takes about two minutes.

I'm new to Notion. Will I be able to use it?

Yes. The 10-page PDF guide walks you through setup step by step, and the dashboard has a built-in "How to use" checklist. Example posts show you exactly what a finished entry looks like.

Can I use it for my clients?

Yes. Duplicate the template once per client so each brand's content stays separate. You can share a client's copy with them for approvals. You can't resell or redistribute the template itself.

Which platforms does it work for?

It comes set up for Instagram, Facebook and TikTok, with separate captions, hashtags and Canva links for feed and TikTok. You can add LinkedIn, YouTube or any other platform in a few clicks.

Does it post for me?

No. Notion is your planning and tracking hub. You'll still publish through the platforms or a scheduler like Metricool, Later or Buffer. The guide includes ideas for connecting them with Make or Zapier.

Can I customise it?

Completely. Rename pillars and topic types, change statuses, add properties, recolor anything. It's your copy.
