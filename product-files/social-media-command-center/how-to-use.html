Step 2 · 30 minutes

Make it yours

Do this once. After that, you only need the weekly rhythm on page 6.

  1. Fill in the Brand HubReplace every [bracketed] placeholder: positioning, audience, voice, words to use and avoid, colors (with HEX codes), fonts and asset links. This is the page you'll share with designers, VAs or clients.
  2. Rename your 5 content pillarsThe template ships with Educate, Show How, Behind the Scenes, Recommend and Engage. To rename: open the Content Calendar, click the Pillar column header → Edit property → click an option to rename it. Do the same in the Idea Bank so the names match, then update the pillar table in the Brand Hub.
  3. Trim the Platforms listClick the Platforms column header → Edit property, and delete the platforms you don't use.
  4. Add your hashtag sets and CTAsIn the Content Playbook, replace the hashtag placeholders and add CTAs that point to your offer.
  5. Load the next 90 days of key datesAdd holidays, sale events (11.11, Black Friday, 12.12), launches and awareness days to Campaigns & Key Dates.
  6. Set up your Tool StackKeep the tools you use, delete the rest, and add costs so you know what social is costing you each month.
  7. Delete the EXAMPLE rowsEach database has sample entries marked EXAMPLE so you can see how a finished post looks. Once you've looked through them, select them and press Delete.
  8. Brain-dump 20 ideasOpen the Idea Bank and add 20 ideas as fast as you can. Don't filter. You'll sort them on Monday.
🔒

Keep these names the same unless you know your way around Notion: Status, Pillar, Publish Date, Reach, Likes, Comments, Saves and Shares. The views, formulas and chart use them. Renaming them is fine; Notion updates references automatically. Deleting them is what breaks things.

Your routine

The pipeline & weekly rhythm

Every post moves left to right through eight statuses. A few focused blocks a week keep it moving.

💡 Idea ✍️ Drafting 🎨 Designing 📸 Needs assets 👀 In review ✅ Ready ⏰ Scheduled 🚀 Published

Needs assets and In review are "blocked" statuses. Anything in them shows up in the Needs Attention view so it doesn't get forgotten.

The weekly rhythm (about 4½ hours a week)

WhenWhat to do
Monday · 30 minPlan. Shortlist ideas in the Idea Bank. Add the best ones to the calendar as 💡 Idea with a date, pillar and format. Check Campaigns for anything coming up.
Tuesday · 90 minWrite. Batch hooks, slide copy and captions using the Playbook. Move posts to ✍️ Drafting, then 🎨 Designing.
Wednesday · 90 minDesign. Create graphics and videos, paste the Design Link. Anything missing a photo goes to 📸 Needs assets and onto your shot list.
Thursday · 30 minQA and schedule. Run the QA checklist, tick QA Passed, load into your scheduler and move to ⏰ Scheduled.
Friday · 15 minMeasure. For posts that went live 7 days ago, log Reach, Likes, Comments, Saves, Shares, Link Clicks and New Followers.
Month end · 30 minReview. Fill in the month in Monthly Performance and answer the five review questions.
⏱️

Short on time? Merge Tuesday–Thursday into one 3-hour batch day. The pipeline still works; you'll just move cards further in one sitting.

Create faster

Writing a post with the Playbook

Open the Content Playbook side by side with your post. Here's the five-minute path from blank page to ready-to-design.

  1. Pick a hook formulaThe Hook Bank has 20 formulas in five groups: curiosity, mistakes, results, how-to and POV. Fill in the brackets and write 3 versions. Keep the best one in the Hook field.
  2. Write the bodyCarousels: one idea per slide, 6–10 slides, big text. Reels: hook on screen in the first 2 seconds, then the value, then the CTA. Put it in Slide / Script Copy.
  3. Build the captionUse the six-part structure: Hook → Context → Value → Proof → CTA → Hashtags. There's a copy-paste template in the Playbook.
  4. Choose exactly one CTAMatch it to the goal. Want reach? Ask for shares. Want saves? Ask for saves. Want leads? Use a comment keyword.
  5. Paste the hashtag setUse the core set plus the right topic set. Don't write hashtags from scratch every time.
  6. Write the Visual BriefOne or two lines your future self (or a designer) can work from, including any photo to shoot.

Repurpose your winners

When a post does well, run the Repurposing workflow in the Playbook: one strong idea becomes 3 short videos, 1 carousel, 2 quote graphics, a text post and a story poll. Add each piece to the Idea Bank with Source set to Repurpose long-form.

Run QA before anything gets scheduled

The 11-point Pre-Publish QA checklist catches the mistakes that cost reach: weak hook, two CTAs, missing captions on video, unreadable cover, broken links. Run it, then tick QA Passed.

✦

Pro tip: paste the Brand Hub's tone of voice and a few of your best captions into your AI writing tool before asking it for drafts. The output will sound much more like you.

Tracking results

Ten minutes of logging a week tells you exactly what to make more of.

Per post (every Friday)

Open the Results view. For each post published about 7 days ago, copy these numbers from the platform's insights: Reach, Likes, Comments, Saves, Shares, Link Clicks and New Followers. If a post went to several platforms, add them together or log your main platform only. Just be consistent.

The dashboard calculates the rest:

Engagements = Likes + Comments + Saves + Shares
Engagement Rate (%) = Engagements ÷ Reach × 100

Sort the Results view by Engagement Rate or Saves to see your best posts instantly.

Per month (last working day)

  1. Fill in the month's rowIn Monthly Performance, add total posts, reach, engagements, saves, shares, clicks, new followers, total followers and leads/DMs. Engagement rate is calculated.
  2. Link your top postUse the Top Post field to link the month's best performer from the calendar.
  3. Answer the five review questionsOpen the month's row. Each one has the questions ready: what worked, which pillar lagged, what the audience asked for, what to stop, and the one experiment for next month.
  4. Check the Pillar Mix chartIf one pillar is way over or under your target %, adjust next month's plan.

The supporting cast

Ideas, campaigns & tools

💡 Idea Bank

Add ideas the moment you have them, even from your phone. Tag the Source (comment, client question, trend…) so you learn where your best ideas come from. On Monday, move the best to Shortlisted, add them to the calendar, then mark them Moved to calendar.

🎯 Campaigns & Key Dates

Add each campaign with a date range, goal and offer. The Timeline view shows what's coming. When you create a post that supports a campaign, set its Campaign field; open the campaign to see all linked posts.

🧰 Tool Stack

Track every tool, what plan you're on and what it costs. Store which email an account is under, never passwords. Review it quarterly and cancel what you don't use.

📸 Shot list

The yellow box at the top of the dashboard is your weekly shot list. When a post is Needs assets, add the photo or clip here so you can shoot everything in one session.

Power-user tips

  • Mobile capture: add the Idea Bank to Favorites in the Notion app and log ideas in seconds.
  • Hide fields you don't use: open any post → ••• → Customize page and hide empty or unused properties.
  • Post templates: in the Content Calendar, click the arrow next to New → New template to save a pre-filled post (e.g. "Weekly carousel") with your usual hashtags and CTA.
  • Client approvals: share a client's copy with them as "Can comment". They comment on the post, you move it out of 👀 In review.
  • Automate logging: with Make or Zapier, you can create a calendar row automatically when a post is published in your scheduler.

Help

FAQ & troubleshooting

Do I need a paid Notion plan?

No. Everything works on the free plan. Notion's free plan has a file-upload size limit, so for big videos paste a Drive or Canva link instead of uploading.

I deleted a view, property or row by accident.

Press Ctrl/Cmd + Z straight away. For deleted pages, check Trash in the sidebar. You can also re-duplicate the original template any time from your link.

The Engagement Rate shows 0.

It needs a Reach number above zero. Fill in Reach and it'll calculate.

A post isn't showing on the Calendar view.

It needs a Publish Date. Undated posts still appear in Pipeline and All Posts.

The Pillar Mix chart looks wrong after I renamed pillars.

Make sure you renamed the existing options rather than creating new ones. Old posts keep the old option until you change them.

Can I use this for more than one brand or client?

Yes. Duplicate the template once per brand. Your licence covers your own brands and the clients you manage.
