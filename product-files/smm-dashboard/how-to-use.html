Step 2 · 30 minutes

Make it yours

Do this once. After that, you only need the weekly rhythm on page 6.

  1. Fill in the Brand HubReplace every [bracketed] placeholder: brand name, positioning, story, offer, audience, voice, words to use and avoid, colors (with HEX codes), fonts, Canva folder links, posting schedule and handles.
  2. Set your 5 content pillarsThe template ships with Educate, Show How, Behind the Scenes, Recommend and Engage. Update the Content Pillars table, then rename the matching options in the Content Calendar: open the calendar, click the Pillar column header → Edit property → click an option to rename it.
  3. Adjust Topic Types and PlatformsDo the same for Topic Type (Tips & How-to, Myth vs Fact, Tutorial…) and Platforms. It comes with Instagram, Facebook and TikTok; add LinkedIn, YouTube or others if you use them.
  4. Add your hashtag sets and CTAsOpen the Hashtag sets and CTA bank toggles and replace the placeholders with your own.
  5. Add upcoming eventsList the next 90 days of key dates in the blue Upcoming events box. There's a list of ideas inside the toggle.
  6. Set up Marketing ToolsAdd the tools you use with their links. Add a logo to Files & media so it shows on the gallery card.
  7. Delete the EXAMPLE postsThe calendar has 8 sample posts marked EXAMPLE so you can see how finished entries look. Open a couple, then select them all and press Delete.
  8. Brain-dump ideasFill the Idea Parking Lot at the bottom with as many ideas as you can. Don't filter. You'll sort them on Monday.
🔒

Rename, don't delete. The views use Date, Status, Pillar and the two Graphics fields. Renaming any property is fine; Notion updates the views automatically. Deleting them is what breaks things.

Your routine

Statuses & weekly rhythm

Every post moves through seven statuses. A few focused blocks a week keep it moving.

Not started Copy drafted Designed Needs photo Ready to post Scheduled Published

Needs photo means the design is done but it's waiting on a photo (for example, a TikTok background). Add the shot to the Content needed box so you can take them all in one session.

The weekly rhythm (about 4½ hours a week)

WhenWhat to do
Monday · 30 minPlan. Pick ideas from the Idea Parking Lot. Add them to the calendar as Not started with a date, week, pillar and topic type. Check Upcoming events.
Tuesday · 90 minWrite. Batch hooks, slide copy, captions and CTAs using the Hook & Caption Bank. Move posts to Copy drafted.
Wednesday · 90 minDesign. Make the graphics in Canva, paste the links and upload the exports. Move to Designed, or Needs photo if you're waiting on a shot.
Thursday · 30 minQA and schedule. Run the Pre-Publish QA Checklist, move to Ready to post, schedule, then move to Scheduled.
Friday · 15 minCheck in. Mark live posts Published and note anything that did well in Notes.
Month end · 30 minReview. Fill in the Monthly Performance Tracker and answer the five review questions.
⏱️

Short on time? Merge Tuesday–Thursday into one 3-hour batch day. The status board still works; you'll just move cards further in one sitting.

Create faster

Writing and designing a post

Open a post and scroll to the Hook & Caption Bank on the dashboard. Here's the path from blank page to ready-to-post.

  1. Pick a hook formulaThe Hook formulas toggle has 15 fill-in-the-blank hooks. Write 3 versions and keep the best in the Hook field.
  2. Write the slides or scriptCarousels: one idea per slide, 6–10 slides, big text. Reels: hook on screen in the first 2 seconds, then the value, then the CTA. Put it in Slide Copy.
  3. Write both captionsUse the six-part Caption structure (or the copy-paste template) for IG/FB. Then write a one- or two-line version for TikTok.
  4. Choose exactly one CTAMatch it to the goal from the CTA bank: shares for reach, saves for value, a comment keyword for leads.
  5. Paste the hashtagsUse the core set plus the right topic set. Follow the usage rule in the toggle for each platform.
  6. Brief and designWrite a Visual Brief, design in Canva using the sizes in the Platform size cheat sheet, paste the Canva links and upload the exports to the Graphics fields.

Repurpose your winners

When a post does well, run the Repurposing Workflow: one strong idea becomes 3 short videos, 1 carousel, 2 quote graphics, a text post and a story poll. Add each piece to the calendar.

Run QA before anything gets scheduled

The 12-point Pre-Publish QA Checklist catches the mistakes that cost reach: a weak hook, two CTAs, missing video captions, an unreadable cover, broken links. Untick the boxes and reuse it for every post.

✦

Pro tip: paste your Brand Hub tone of voice and a few of your best captions into your AI writing tool before asking it for drafts. The output will sound much more like you.

Tracking results

Thirty minutes at the end of each month tells you exactly what to make more of.

The Monthly Performance Tracker

On the last working day of the month, open your platform insights (or your scheduler's analytics) and fill in one row: posts, reach, engagement rate, saves, shares, new followers, leads/DMs and your top post. Rename [Month 1] to the actual month, and add a row each month.

To work out engagement rate:

Engagements = Likes + Comments + Saves + Shares
Engagement rate (%) = Engagements ÷ Reach × 100

Write the top post's name in the last column, and add a note to that post in the calendar about why it worked.

The Monthly Review

  1. Which post performed best, and what drove it?Look at the hook, format and topic. Make more like it.
  2. Which pillar underperformed, and why?Compare against the % targets in your Content Pillars table.
  3. What are comments and DMs asking for?Add those questions to the Idea Parking Lot.
  4. What will you stop doing, and what's your one experiment?Pick one change for next month. Just one.

Power-user tips

  • Mobile capture: add the dashboard to Favorites in the Notion app and log ideas in seconds.
  • Hide fields you don't use: open any post → ••• → Customize page and hide empty or unused properties.
  • Post templates: in the Content Calendar, click the arrow next to New → New template to save a pre-filled post (e.g. "Tuesday carousel") with your usual hashtags and CTA.
  • Client approvals: share a client's copy as "Can comment". They comment on the post; you move it on once it's approved.
  • Automate it: with Make or Zapier you can send a post's caption and graphics to your scheduler when its status changes to Ready to post.

Help

FAQ & troubleshooting

Do I need a paid Notion plan?

No. Everything works on the free plan. Notion's free plan has a file-upload size limit, so for big videos paste a Drive or Canva link instead of uploading.

I deleted a view, property or row by accident.

Press Ctrl/Cmd + Z straight away. For deleted pages, check Trash in the sidebar. You can also re-duplicate the original template any time from your link.

The graphics previews are empty.

They show images uploaded to the IG/FB Graphics and TikTok Graphics fields. Upload your exported PNG or JPG files there.

A post isn't showing on the Calendar view.

It needs a Date. Undated posts still appear in the Status board and the table.

A card disappeared from the Status board after I renamed a status.

Rename the existing option rather than creating a new one, and check that empty groups aren't hidden (••• → Group → Show empty groups).

Can I use this for more than one brand or client?

Yes. Duplicate the template once per brand. Your licence covers your own brands and the clients you manage.
